Topsoil Math keeps coverage, bag and settle arithmetic only. Every yard, bag and settle figure is a labeled norm, not a promise - soil density, moisture and compaction change real loads. Call 811 before any digging, follow the supplier's measure, and never size a vehicle's load from this page - its rated payload lives in the vehicle's manual.

The bag claims three-quarters of a cubic foot it measured loosely. The truck claims a yard it eyeballed. Topsoil Math does the honest arithmetic: how much ground a depth really covers, how many bags that really is, and how much extra to order before the soil settles down.

Three panels. The coverage truth turns square feet and inches into cubic yards. The bag truth counts whole bags against that same ground. The settle truth adds the allowance the labeled settle norm asks for, so the finished inch is the inch you ordered.
